      Frequently asked questions

      Can you work with public or anonymized datasets?

      Yes. Public, simulated, anonymized, or properly authorized research datasets are all fine to start with.

      What do I receive at the end?

      Typical delivery includes result tables, figure exports, reusable scripts or notebooks when appropriate, and a concise methods and results summary.

      Can you interpret clinical or patient-specific findings?

      No. The work is limited to research support, data analysis, visualization, and reproducible reporting. Diagnosis, treatment decisions, and patient-specific interpretation are out of scope.

      What makes a project easier to scope?

      A clear sample sheet, group labels, contrast definition, organism and reference version, expected figures, and any previous analysis decisions.

      Ready to discuss a dataset?

      Send organism, data type, sample groups, contrast, and expected outputs, and say whether you need figures, tables, scripts, or a short report. I usually reply within 24-48 hours.
